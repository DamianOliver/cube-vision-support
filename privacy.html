Cube-Vision Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026 · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

Cube-Vision is developed and operated by Damian Piech. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to the developer of Cube-Vision.

Cube-Vision does not collect your personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking, and it does not send your solves, videos, or any other information to us or to anyone else. What the app records stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

The optional Premium subscription is sold and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple ID, or your payment details.

Information we collect

None. We do not operate a server, and Cube-Vision makes no network requests of its own, other than asking Apple's App Store about subscriptions, as described under "Purchases" below, and checking today's date with Apple's website, as described under "Lessons" below. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is nothing for us to store, analyse, sell, or disclose.

Lessons

Without a Premium subscription, a new lesson becomes available each day. When you start that day's lesson, Cube-Vision checks today's date by contacting Apple's website (apple.com) and reading the date from its reply, once per lesson. The request carries nothing about you, your device, or your progress, and the app sends nothing else.

Information stored on your device

Cube-Vision saves the following in its own private storage on your device, where it is accessible to the app alone:

  • Solve times, scrambles, dates, penalties, and reconstructed move sequences
  • Video recordings of your solves, when you choose to keep them
  • Your lesson progress and trainer results
  • Whether you have an active Premium subscription
  • Your app settings and preferences

Cube-Vision never uploads any of this. It leaves your device only if you deliberately send it somewhere: see "Sharing" below. If you back up your device, these files may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy rather than by us.

Camera

Cube-Vision uses the camera to record your solves, to read your cube and follow your moves during lessons, the trainer, and Solve, and, if you enable timer hand tracking, to detect the position of your hands. Video frames are analysed entirely on your device, by software bundled inside the app, and Cube-Vision never transmits camera data anywhere of its own accord. There is no server for it to go to.

Recordings are saved to your device, and a recording leaves it only if you choose to share or save that solve yourself, as described under "Sharing" and "Photo library" below. Camera access is requested the first time you need it, and you can revoke it at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Cube-Vision offers an optional Premium subscription on iPhone. It is sold, billed, and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Payment happens in Apple's purchase screen, and we never receive your name, your Apple ID, your email address, or any payment details.

To decide whether to unlock Premium, the app asks Apple's StoreKit service on your device about this app's subscriptions for your Apple ID, such as which plan is active and when it ends. The app keeps only whether you are subscribed, on your device, so that Premium keeps working when you are offline. It sends this nowhere.

You can view, change, or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple account's subscription settings, which the app can also open for you. Refunds are requested from and handled by Apple.

Photo library

Cube-Vision does not read your photo library. If you share a solve and choose "Save Video" from the share sheet, iOS asks your permission and saves that one recording to your library. This happens only when you explicitly choose it.

Sharing

Sharing is always something you start. When you share a solve, Cube-Vision hands iOS the solve's details — its time, scramble, and reconstructed moves — together with the video recording of that solve, if one was kept. You then choose the destination: a messaging app, email, AirDrop, or another app on your device. Once you send it, the recording and details are handled by whichever service you chose, under its privacy policy rather than ours.

Shared solves include a "Replay" link to alpha.twizzle.net, a third-party cube-visualisation website, with the scramble and move sequence encoded in the link. Cube-Vision does not open this link itself and sends nothing to that site. If you or the person you shared with opens the link, that site will receive the scramble and moves it contains. It does not contain your name, your device information, or any video.

Children

Cube-Vision is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Retaining and deleting your data

You are in control of everything the app stores. Individual solves and their videos can be deleted from within the app. Deleting Cube-Vision from your device removes all of its data, including every solve record and video.

Because we never receive your data, there is no account to close and no request you need to make to us in order to have it erased. Records of your purchases are kept by Apple, not by us; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, which you can do in your Apple account's subscription settings.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the "last updated" date above will change. If a future version of the app ever collects or transmits data, that change will be described here before it takes effect.
